Apple Cider Donuts
Yield: 14 donuts


* Flour 1 ¾ c.
* Baking powder 1 ¼ tsp
* Salt ¾ tsp
* Cinnamon 1 tsp
* Nutmeg ½ tsp
* Butter 10 Tbsp
* Brown sugar ¾ c
* Sugar ¼ c
* Eggs 2 each
* Vanilla 1 tsp
* Apple cider 1/2c, warm




1. Preheat oven to 350℉
2. Cream butter with brown sugar and sugar until light
3. Add eggs and vanilla slowly
4. Combine dry ingredients
5. Alternate adding dry ingredients and apple cider to butter mixture
6. Spray donut pans
7. Use piping bag to fill pans
8. Bake 6 mins, rotate pans, and bake 6 more mins
9. Remove from pans and toss in sugar mixture
10. Portion into bags and put into containers




Sugar Mixture
* Sugar 1/2c
* Cinnamon 1 tsp
* Cardamom ¼ tsp






Leige Waffles
Yield: 45 waffles


* Flour 8c
* Cinnamon 1 ½ tsp
* Salt 4 ½ tsp
* Milk 2 c, warm
* Active dry yeast 1 Tbsp
* Sugar 4 Tbsp
* Eggs 4 each
* Vanilla 2 Tbsp
* Butter 2c 
* Pearl sugar 2 c


1. Combine dry ingredients
2. Combine warm milk, sugar, and yeast. Let rise in warm place until foamy
3. Add yeast mixture, eggs, and vanilla to mixer and mix with dough hook until ball forms
4. Mix on 2 for 3 mins
5. Slowly add butter while mixing 
6. Once butter is incorporated, place dough in hotel pan and cover with plastic wrap
7. Allow to rise in a warm place for 30 mins
8. After 30 mins, knead in pearl sugar
9. Divide into 2 oz balls
10. Put dough balls back into hotel pan and cover. Allow to rise 1 hour
11. Cook in waffle iron at 350 ℉ for 30 sec each
12. Cool and portion into bags and place in containers

Candied Pecans
* Sugar ½ c
* Brown sugar ½ c
* Cinnamon 4 tsp
* Salt 1 tsp
* Egg whites 1 ½ Tbsp
* Vanilla extract 1 tsp
* Pecans 12 oz


1. Preheat oven to 300 ℉
2. Combine dry ingredients
3. Whisk egg whites and vanilla until soft peaks form
4. Fold pecans into the egg whites until they are evenly coated
5. Mix in the dry ingredients until pecans are all covered
6. Spread onto sheet trays and bake for 20 mins
7. Rotate trays in the oven and stir pecans 
8. Bake for an additional 20 mins
9. Allow to cool and put into a container in dry storage















































Puff Pastry
Yield: 50 squares for BEC
   * Flour 6 c
   * Sugar 1 Tbsp
   * Salt 2 tsp
   * Butter 1.5 lbs
   * Ice water 2 c


   1. Add flour, salt, and sugar to bowl of stand mixer
   2. Cut butter into cubes and add to mixer
   3. Mix until butter is coated by flour (about 1 minute)
   4. Add water slowly (without ice) 
   5. Mix until dough forms
   6. Roll out into a rectangle
   7. Do one letter fold (fold into 3rd like a business letter)
   8. Rotate 90 degrees and roll out into a rectangle again
   9. Do a book fold (fold edges to center and then fold in half)
   10. Rotate 90 degrees again and do another book fold
   11. Wrap and refrigerate for 30 mins
   12. Roll into a rectangle and cut in half
   13. Roll each half out to ¼ inch thick 
   14. Use dough cutter wheel to cut into squares
   15. Repeat with other half
   16. Egg wash and chill for 30 mins
   17. Bake at 350 ℉ for 15 mins, rotate in oven and bake for 5 additional mins
   18. Cool 


















Bacon, Egg, and Cheese
Yield: 50 BEC


   * Eggs 45 each
   * Milk 2.5 c
   * Salt 1 ½ Tbsp
   * Pepper ½ Tbsp
   * Bacon 3 layers (frozen bacon)
   * Cheddar cheese 1 c


   1. Cut bacon into small pieces and cook in large skillet
   2. Whisk together eggs, milk, salt, and pepper
   3. Add egg mixture to pan with bacon
   4. Cook while stirring constantly until scrambled eggs are cooked
   5. Turn off heat and stir in cheese
   6. Cool in refrigerator
   7. Once cooled, use red handled scoop to scoop eggs onto puff pastry squares
   8. Sprinkle with cheese and portion into bags and put into containers

Apple Turnovers
Yield: 28 each

   * Sugar ⅔ c
   * Cinnamon 2 tsp
   * Cardamom ¼ tsp
   * Lemon juice 2 tsp
   * Water ¾ c
   * Cornstarch 8 tsp

   4. Cook until apples are tender, stirring occasionally
   5. Add slurry and stir until mixture becomes thick
   6. Allow to cool


Puff Pastry 
   * Flour 6 c

   * Salt 2 tsp
   * Butter 1.5 lbs
   * Ice water 2 c


   19. Add flour, salt, and sugar to bowl of stand mixer
   20. Cut butter into cubes and add to mixer
   21. Mix until butter is coated by flour (about 1 minute)
   22. Add water slowly (without ice) 
   23. Mix until dough forms
   24. Roll out into a rectangle
   25. Do one letter fold (fold into 3rd like a business letter)
   26. Rotate 90 degrees and roll out into a rectangle again
   27. Do a book fold (fold edges to center and then fold in half)
   28. Rotate 90 degrees again and do another book fold
   29. Wrap and refrigerate for 30 mins
   30. Roll into a rectangle and cut in half
   31. Roll each half out to ¼ inch thick 
   32. Use dough cutter wheel to cut into squares

Icing
   * Powdered sugar 1 ½ c
   * Water 3 TBSP
   * Salt ⅛ tsp
   * Vanilla extract ½ tsp


   1. Whisk all ingredients together until smooth






Assembling the Turnover
   1. Roll out puff pastry to ¼ inch thick
   2. Use pastry cutter to cut into squares
   3. Use 1 oz scoop to put apple filling in the center of each square
   4. Egg wash edges with a pastry brush
   5. Fold puff pastry over to create a triangle shape
   6. Crimp edges with a fork to seal
   7. Make 3 slits in the top for vent holes using a knife
   8. Chill for 10 minutes
   9. Egg wash top of turnovers
   10. Bake at 325 in convection oven for 25 minutes rotating halfway through
   11. Let cool
   12. Drizzle with icing

Maple Brown Sugar Scones
Yield: 8 each
Expo: 4 days


   * Flour 2 ½ c
   * Baking Powder 1 TBSP
   * Salt 1 tsp
   * Brown Sugar ⅓ c
   * Butter 6 TBSP
   * Vanilla extract ½ tsp
   * Milk 1 c
   1. Combine dry ingredients in bowl of mixer
   2. Add butter and mix until butter cubes are about dime size (1st speed)
   3. Add milk and vanilla and mix until a dough forms (1st speed)
   4. Weigh into 738 g balls and press dough into a circle about 1 inches thick
   5. Cut into 8 wedges (like cutting a pie)
   6. Place wedges on sheet tray and sprinkle tops with brown sugar
   7. Chill for 10 mins
   8. Bake at 400 F for 10-15 mins 
   9. Chill and drizzle with glaze


Maple glaze
   * Powdered sugar 1  c
   * Milk 1/2 TBSP
   * Maple syrup 2 TBSP
   * Maple extract 1/2 TBSP


   1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and whisk until smooth
   2. Drizzle over cooled scones
