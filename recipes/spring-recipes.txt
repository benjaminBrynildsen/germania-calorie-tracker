Lemon Syrup
Yield: 1 syrup bottle
Expo: 6 days


* Sugar 625 g
* Water 312 g
* Lemon extract 3.75 TBSP




1. Heat water 
2. Add water, sugar, and extract to a pot and blend with immersion blender until dissolved
3. Bottle 




























































Haus White Mocha 
Yield: 1 sauce bottle
Expo: 6 days


* Sugar 767 g
* Water 630 g
* Vanilla bean 1 each 
* Fructose 240 g
* Nonfat dry milk 90 g
* Soy lecithin 6 g
* Cocoa butter 150 g
* Salt 0.33 tsp




1. Heat water
2. Blend portion of water with vanilla bean and strain
3. Combine water, sugar, fructose, salt, nonfat dry milk, and soy lecithin in a pot and mix with immersion blender until combined and dissolved
4. Melt cocoa butter
5. Slowly add cocoa butter to pot and mix thoroughly with immersion blender
6. Bottle














































Horchata Syrup 
Yield: 1 syrup bottle
Expo: 6 days




* Cinnamon sticks 4 each
* Water 20 oz 
* Rice 280 g
* Vanilla extract 1 TBSP
* Sugar 20 oz (volume)


1. Blend all ingredients besides sugar
2. Strain through a cloth cold brew bag
3. Mix with sugar and blend again to dissolve sugar
4. Bottle into syrup bottles 


























































Lavender Syrup
Yield: 1 syrup bottle
Expo: 6 days
* Sugar 625g
* Water 500 g
* Lavender buds 35 g


1. Heat water to 160F 
2. Add lavender and steep 6 mins
3. Strain
4. Weigh water amount and add double in sugar (roughly 625g)
5. Blend until sugar is dissolved
